### What this means for the fold-in (backlog #1)
A regular line is a catalogue SKU with size, qty, a quoted price (B2B price by default, overridable), a per-line discount, a client note and a Completed flag; there is no costing sheet in the browser (the cost sits behind the "Cost" form). In the unified model this is a ring with product kind "machine-made band": the Custom Bridal sections that do not apply (center stone, side diamonds, CAD fee…) are hidden and the price comes from the catalogue instead of the totals table, with the same lock / send / summary flow.
